# Design guidance research

Reviewed 14 November 2026. The local guidance uses independently written instructions;
the linked articles and standards remain with their respective rights holders.

| Evidence | Applied change |
| --- | --- |
| [Usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) | Clear status, familiar labels, recognition, recovery and removal of irrelevant information |
| [Visual hierarchy](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/) | Position, size, weight and grouping establish the task order |
| [Target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) | Distinguish 24px AA minimum and its exceptions from a 44px product touch target |
| [Focus visibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html) | Test focused controls under sticky content and overlays |
| [Error summaries](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/) | Preserve inputs, link summary messages to fields and give actionable corrections |

For interface craft, the complete pinned Unslop skill is available at
